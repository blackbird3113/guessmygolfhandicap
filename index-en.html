Guess My Golf Handicap

How many strokes you get on this course, and what your handicap will be after the round. With the Italian golf courses already inside.

What it does

Course handicap

Pick the club, the course and the colour of the tees: the app works out the strokes you receive with the official World Handicap System formula. The Italian golf courses are already inside the app, with the Course Rating and Slope of every set of tees.

Your new handicap after the round

The official figure arrives the next day. Enter your Stableford points and you know at once how your Handicap Index will move: the average of the best 8 differentials of the last 20, the table for an incomplete record, the exceptional score reduction, soft cap and hard cap.

The calculation is not a black box

Every step is visible: which eight differentials are making your handicap, their sum, the division, the rounding and the adjustments. And a full guide explains the handicap system with all the formulas.

Works with no connection and no account. The course data is inside the app, and your scores stay on your phone.

A note about the numbers

The figure worked out here is a prediction. The official one can differ by a tenth or two if a PCC adjustment is applied, which is determined after the competition from the scores of everyone who played and cannot be known beforehand by anyone.

The app is not an official product of any golf federation, nor connected with one. The course parameters are public data.
